Time points

Both players start at 15 points. The faster player gains points and the slower player loses the same amount. Maximum difference: 15 points.

Time A = 15 + 7.5 × (Time B − Time A) ÷ (Time A + Time B)
Time B = 30 − Time A

The round ends only when both players solve the word or use all six guesses. Your timer stops when your own game ends.
